About Me

Hey there! 👋 I'm Bingxu An (安炳旭), an M.S. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, majoring in Electronic Engineering (EE). I received my B.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence from Huaqiao University (HQU) in July 2025.

Since May 2026, I have been a research intern at ModelBest (面壁智能), working on LLM post-training and data synthesis. I'm a core contributor to MiniCPM5-2B.

At HQU I joined the SENDS Lab, where I met a group of incredible friends who share the same passion for technology and innovation. Now, my research passion lies in LLM post-training, data synthesis, and agentic RL.

Just feel free to reach out via email or GitHub! 🤝
